## 2. iPhone label PDF error

- **Cause:** the PDF maker is loaded only when needed (a separate file of the app's build). When the hub got a new build (deploy) while the app was open on the phone, the new service worker deleted the old build's files from its cache. The page still ran the old build, asked for the old PDF file, and the hub answered with its start page (HTML). Safari then said "'text/html' is not a valid JavaScript MIME type".
- **Fix:** (a) `client/src/main.js`: on Vite's `vite:preloadError` the app reloads itself once (at most once a minute), which opens the new build; (b) `client/src/sw-template.js`: the service worker keeps the previous build's cache until the next update, so an open page can still load its parts.

## 3. Wrong price on a vape pod label

- **Not a data error:** all 10 vape pod labels in batch 8 carry $19.99.
- **Cause:** each label put the name at its top edge and the price at its bottom edge, with empty space between. On the sheet, a price ended up right above the next row's name. The vape pod label below "Paper towels 6 rolls" therefore read as $10.99, and with any print offset or scaling, that price lands on the next label. That makes exactly one vape label "wrong", as reported.
- **Fix:** `client/src/lib/labels/pdf.js`: name, details and price are one block centred in the label. Checked by redrawing batch 8 (before/after images in the session).
