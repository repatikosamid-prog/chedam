### How the memory problem was found

The spike came after selling, while the test read thousands of records. Measured on the Pi, sampling 5 times a second: stock movements 500 a page → 232 MB, 100 a page → 104 MB; sale lines (cost hook) 215 MB; audit log (no hook) 160 MB. On the laptop as a manager: 1,194 sale lines → 51 → 416 MB in 4.3 s; audit log 7,374 rows → 105 MB in 0.8 s. The cost-hiding hook loaded the whole access library and asked the database for the person's role and overrides for every record.
